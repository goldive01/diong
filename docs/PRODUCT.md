# Diong Product Definition

## Product Vision

Diong is a personal-development social platform that helps people prepare their attention, take meaningful action, build consistency, and reflect on their progress.

The product is centred on structured Prime Protocols, purposeful Action Triggers, personal goals, habits, private journaling, and supportive community interaction.

Primary tagline: Prime your mind. Act on your goals. Become more.

## Problem Being Solved

Many people know what they want to improve, but struggle to convert intention into repeatable action. Common problems include unclear priorities, inconsistent habits, scattered reflection, and social spaces that reward performance more than steady progress.

Diong gives users a simple daily structure:

- read a focused Prime Protocol;
- complete one practical Action Trigger;
- track goals and habits;
- reflect privately in a journal;
- share selected progress with a supportive community.

## Target Users

- People struggling with consistency.
- People working toward personal goals.
- Students and learners.
- Career changers.
- Entrepreneurs.
- Professionals interested in personal development.

## Value Proposition

Diong helps users turn personal-development intent into a calm daily practice. It combines short reflective guidance, clear actions, progress tracking, and selective community support without presenting itself as therapy, coaching, or a guaranteed-success system.

## Brand Identity

Name: Diong

Tagline: Prime your mind. Act on your goals. Become more.

Brand personality:

- calm;
- purposeful;
- intelligent;
- encouraging;
- modern;
- trustworthy;
- inclusive;
- action-oriented.

Diong should not feel like:

- a generic quote website;
- a medical treatment platform;
- a loud motivational-poster website;
- an Instagram clone;
- a cryptocurrency product;
- a mystical or supernatural service.

## Product Principles

- Practical over performative: encourage small actions users can actually complete.
- Reflection before reaction: use Prime Protocols to support attention, reflection, motivation, habits, and purposeful action.
- Private by default: journals, goals, habits, and completions should remain private unless a user deliberately shares something.
- Community with boundaries: social features should support encouragement and accountability, not comparison or pressure.
- Realistic scope: prioritise dependable core workflows over broad feature expansion.
- Clear safety language: avoid medical, neurological, mental-health treatment, or guaranteed outcome claims.
- Accessible and mobile-first: core daily actions should work well on small screens.

## Success Indicators

- Users complete registration and onboarding.
- Users return to read Daily Prime Protocols.
- Users complete Action Triggers.
- Users create goals and habits.
- Users log habit completions over multiple days.
- Users write private journal entries.
- Users follow others, post selectively, and interact respectfully.
- Users manage account settings without support.

These indicators measure product engagement and usefulness. They must not be presented as proof of personal transformation, medical improvement, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Important Limitations And Safety Language

Diong is a personal-development product, not a medical, mental-health, neurological, or crisis-support service.

Prime Protocols should be described as tools for attention, reflection, motivation, habits, and purposeful action. Diong must not claim to rewire the brain, treat conditions, replace professional support, or guarantee life outcomes.

AI personalisation is planned for a later release. It must not provide mental-health advice, diagnosis, crisis guidance, or medical recommendations.
